I am a first-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at the HKUST NLP Group, supervised by Professor Junxian He. I graduated with a B.Eng. from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University(SJTU)](https://www.shanghaijiaotong.edu.cn) in June 2024. My research focuses on [natural language processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_language_processing) and [machine learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machine_learning).

## Academic Background

### Education
- [Ph.D. in Computer Science](https://www.ece.ust.hk/), Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), 2024-Present, advised by [Professor Junxian He](https://www.ece.ust.hk/people/my_he_junxian.html)
- B.Eng. in Electronic Information Science and Technology, Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), June 2024. Graduated with the [Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship](https://www.shanghaijiaotong.edu.cn/) (Undergraduate)
