I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)](https://www.ust.hk/), where I am a member of the [HKUST NLP Group](https://nlp.ust.hk/) led by Professor [Junxian He](https://faculty.ust.hk/junxian-he/), my Ph.D. supervisor. I graduated from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/) in June 2024 with a B.Eng. in Computer Science, where I was advised by Professor Junxian He.

## Research

My research focuses on **natural language processing** and **machine learning**. I am specifically interested in:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLMs)**
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**

## Academic Background

## Publications

I am a first author on several peer-reviewed publications:

1. **SynLogic: Synthesizing Verifiable Reasoning Data at Scale for Learning Logical Reasoning and Beyond** (2025). Published on arXiv. First author. Code repository available on GitHub. Co-authors: Yuanxiang Fan, Zhuo Jiang, Han Ding, Yongyi Hu, Chi Zhang, Yiqi Shi, Shitong Weng, Aili Chen, Shiqi Chen, Yunan Huang, Mozhi Zhang, Pengyu Zhao, Junjie Yan, Junxian He.
2. **On the Perception Bottleneck of VLMs for Chart Understanding** (2025). Published on arXiv. First author. Code repository: [Vision4Chart](https://github.com/Vicent0205/Vision4Chart). Co-authors: Weihao Zeng, Xiwen Zhang, Yijun Wang, Zifei Shan, Junxian He.
3. **On the Universal Truthfulness Hyperplane Inside LLMs** (2024). Published at [EMNLP 2024](https://2024.emnlp.org/). First author. Code repository: [Universal_Truthfulness_Hyperplane](https://github.com/Vicent0205/Universal_Truthfulness_Hyperplane). Co-authors: Shiqi Chen, Yu Cheng, Junxian He.

I have also contributed as a co-author to the following works:

4. **In-Context Sharpness as Alerts: An Inner Representation Perspective for Hallucination Mitigation** (2024). Published at [ICML 2024](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2024). Co-authors: Shiqi Chen, Miao Xiong, Zhengxuan Wu, Teng Xiao, Siyang Gao, Junxian He.
5. **C-Eval: A Multi-Level Multi-Discipline Chinese Evaluation Suite for Foundation Models** (2023). Published at [NeurIPS 2023](https://nips.cc/Conferences/2023). Co-authors: Yuzhen Huang, Yuzhuo Bai, Zhihao Zhu, Junlei Zhang, Jinghan Zhang, Tangjun Su, Chuancheng Lv, Yikai Zhang, Jiayi Lei, Yao Fu, Maosong Sun, Junxian He.
6. **Composing Parameter-Efficient Modules with Arithmetic Operations** (2023). Published at [NeurIPS 2023](https://nips.cc/Conferences/2023). Co-authors: Jinghan Zhang, Shiqi Chen, Junxian He.
